Test data for `test_eratv.py`.

- `*.xml`: ERATV XML exports of four vehicle types, downloaded on 2026-11-10 from
  https://eratv.era.europa.eu/ (source: ERATV, © European Union Agency for Railways;
  reproduction authorised with acknowledgement of the source). The contact data of the
  manufacturer and the authorisation holder is replaced by "(removed)".
- `list.html`: the first page of ERATV's list of authorisations, the same day.
- `vocab.ttl`: the part of the ERA ontology (EUPL 1.2) and ERA's SKOS lists (CC BY 4.0) the
  conversion uses, from the `rinf/ontology` and `rinf/skos` graphs of the rinf-plus repository
  (see third_party/).
